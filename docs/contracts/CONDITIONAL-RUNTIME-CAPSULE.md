## Integration seams and open gates

The four existing source seams are python-engine, transport, protected Worker and
its manifest row. The engine adds a trusted shared-import registry after its verified
core/admission and lazy manual/predictor imports. Root merges these additively with
quality_inspect. Both initialization orders, combined diagnostic cache + capsule
residency, protected-advisory/foreground priority and cross-operation termination/
stale-fatal ownership need the separate combined candidate gate. Individual receipts
are not a combined peak-memory proof. Current app/catalog/short states/BackUndo/index
remain base bytes. No conditional UI/Apply/export is wired by this slice.

Automatic personal best-stop remains unidentified under currently available inputs
and this model. Clock/service break-even is only a limited supporting design, not
completion or replacement of that requirement, and must not be called recovery
prediction. Scientific/local-history admission and C12 execution remain separate.
